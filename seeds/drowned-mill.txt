A fen village built around a half-sunken watermill. The miller vanished
during the spring flood and the mill wheel still turns at night, though the
race is dammed. Tone: damp, quiet folk horror, small kindnesses. Factions:
the eel-trappers who want the mill reopened, and the reeve who wants it
burned. I play a journeyman carpenter who arrived to repair the sluice gate.
